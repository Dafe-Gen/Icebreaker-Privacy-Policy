# Icebreaker Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 24, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 24, 2026

Icebreaker is a venue-based social networking application developed and operated by **Nexsewa**, based in Nepal.

This Privacy Policy explains how Icebreaker collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Icebreaker mobile application and related services.

By creating an account or using Icebreaker, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 1. About Icebreaker

Icebreaker helps users discover nearby venues, join a venue-based room, view eligible users at the same venue, send and receive waves, chat, and voluntarily arrange in-person meetings.

Icebreaker does not guarantee that another user is genuine, physically present at a venue, safe, or suitable to meet.

## 2. Minimum Age

You must be at least **16 years old** to use Icebreaker.

Users aged 16 or 17 may use Icebreaker only with permission from a parent or legal guardian and where permitted by applicable law.

Users under 16 must not create or use an account. If we learn that an underage user has created an account, we may suspend the account and delete or anonymize the associated information.

Icebreaker may request reasonable information to confirm a user’s age and may restrict interactions where necessary for user safety.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Account information

We may collect:

* Mobile phone number;
* One-time password verification information;
* Internal account identifier;
* Account creation date;
* Login and authentication records; and
* Account status.

### 3.2 Profile and onboarding information

We may collect information provided during onboarding or profile editing, including:

* Name or display name;
* Date of birth and age;
* Profile photographs and uploaded images;
* Gender, where voluntarily provided;
* Interests;
* Socializing preferences;
* Preferred activities or venue categories;
* Profile description; and
* Other information voluntarily added to a profile.

Some profile information may be visible to other eligible users.

Do not upload another person’s image or personal information without permission.

### 3.3 Location and venue information

Icebreaker uses location information to provide its main features. We may collect:

* Approximate location;
* Precise device location when permission is granted;
* Latitude and longitude;
* Location accuracy;
* Nearby venue searches;
* Venues selected or joined;
* Venue joining and checkout times;
* Venue-presence status; and
* Location-related security and technical records.

Other eligible users may see that you have joined the same venue. Icebreaker does not intend to display your exact coordinates publicly.

You may disable location access in your device settings, but map, nearby venue, and venue-room features may stop working.

### 3.4 Waves and interactions

We may collect:

* Waves sent and received;
* Wave acceptance, rejection, cancellation, or expiration;
* Chat and connection status;
* Blocking and reporting activity;
* Venue-room interactions; and
* Related timestamps.

### 3.5 Messages

We collect and store messages sent through Icebreaker, including:

* Message content;
* Sender and recipient identifiers;
* Message timestamps;
* Delivery and read status, where supported;
* Reply relationships; and
* Report or moderation status.

Messages are generally visible only to the sender and intended recipient. Authorized personnel may access relevant messages when reasonably necessary to investigate reports, prevent harm or abuse, provide support, enforce the Terms of Service, resolve technical issues, or comply with applicable law.

Recipients may copy, screenshot, record, or redistribute messages. Icebreaker cannot fully prevent this.

### 3.6 Safety and report information

When a user reports or blocks another user, we may collect:

* Reporting and reported account identifiers;
* Report reason;
* Additional comments or evidence;
* Related messages or interactions;
* Relevant venue and time information; and
* Actions taken in response.

### 3.7 Device and technical information

We may automatically collect limited technical information needed to operate and protect Icebreaker, including:

* Device type and operating system;
* App version;
* IP address;
* Device or installation identifier;
* Push-notification token;
* Language and time zone;
* Login records;
* Crash and error records;
* Security and server logs; and
* Network and performance information.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

* Create and authenticate accounts;
* Verify age eligibility;
* Create and display profiles;
* Show nearby venues;
* Allow users to join venue rooms;
* Display eligible users at the same venue;
* Process waves and connections;
* Deliver messages and notifications;
* Provide customer support;
* Process blocks and reports;
* Prevent fraud, fake accounts, spam, harassment, exploitation, and security threats;
* Enforce our Terms of Service and safety rules;
* Maintain and improve app performance;
* Protect users, Nexsewa, and the public;
* Comply with applicable law; and
* Respond to valid legal requests.

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Permissions

Icebreaker may request permission for:

* Location;
* Camera;
* Photo library;
* Push notifications; and
* Other device features required for an optional app function.

You may withdraw device permissions through your phone settings. Some features may no longer work after permission is withdrawn.

## 6. Information Visible to Other Users

Depending on the feature and your profile, other eligible users may see:

* Display name;
* Profile photographs;
* Age or age range;
* Interests and preferences;
* Profile description;
* Venue-presence status;
* Wave or connection status; and
* Messages sent to them.

Your phone number, exact GPS coordinates, authentication information, and internal account identifier are not intended to be publicly displayed.

Do not add your home address, passwords, financial details, identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information to your profile or messages.

## 7. How We Share Information

### 7.1 Other users

We share profile, venue-presence, wave, connection, and message information as needed to provide features that you choose to use.

### 7.2 Service providers

We may use third-party service providers for services such as:

* Cloud hosting and databases;
* File and image storage;
* Maps and venue information;
* Push notifications;
* OTP delivery;
* Error reporting;
* Security; and
* App analytics.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to Icebreaker and are expected to protect it appropriately.

### 7.3 Legal and safety reasons

We may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

* Comply with applicable law or a valid legal request;
* Investigate suspected illegal conduct;
* Protect a person from serious harm;
* Prevent child exploitation or abuse;
* Enforce our agreements;
* Protect legal rights; or
* Detect and respond to fraud or security incidents.

### 7.4 Business changes

If ownership or operation of Icebreaker changes, relevant information may be transferred as part of that change, subject to applicable privacy and confidentiality obligations.

## 8. Location Privacy

Joining a venue may show other eligible users that you are currently or recently associated with that venue.

You must not use Icebreaker to track, stalk, monitor, or determine another person’s movements.

Icebreaker takes reasonable steps to:

* Avoid publicly displaying exact coordinates;
* End venue presence after checkout or inactivity;
* Allow users to leave a venue room;
* Restrict unauthorized location access; and
* Retain location records only as reasonably necessary.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Icebreaker, protect users, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

Generally:

* Profile information is retained while the account is active;
* Live venue presence is retained until checkout, expiry, or the end of the venue session;
* Messages are retained while needed to provide chat and safety features;
* Reports and moderation records may be retained for safety, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance;
* Security and technical logs are retained for a limited operational period; and
* Deleted information may remain temporarily in protected backups until normal backup rotation is completed.

We may retain anonymized information that no longer reasonably identifies a user.

## 10. Account Deletion

Users may request permanent account deletion through the account settings available in the app.

Users may also contact **[info@nexsewa.com.np](mailto:info@nexsewa.com.np)** for privacy or deletion support.

After a valid deletion request:

* The account and profile will be removed or anonymized;
* Active sessions and venue presence will be ended;
* Associated profile data, images, location records, and other personal information will be deleted or anonymized, except where retention is reasonably required for security, fraud prevention, abuse investigations, dispute resolution, or legal compliance;
* Messages and other user content associated with the account will be deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible, subject to legal and safety requirements; and
* Backup copies may remain until normal backup rotation is completed, generally within 90 days.

Account deletion is different from temporary deactivation. Icebreaker will not describe a temporarily disabled account as permanently deleted.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, which may include:

* Encryption during transmission;
* Authentication controls;
* Restricted system access;
* Access and security logs;
* Software and security updates;
* Backup protections; and
* Incident-response procedures.

No electronic system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or misuse will never occur.

You are responsible for protecting your device, phone number, and verification codes.

## 13. User Safety

Online and in-person interactions involve risks. Users should:

* Meet in a public place;
* Inform a trusted person before meeting someone;
* Maintain control of transportation;
* Avoid sharing financial or identification information;
* Stop communicating with anyone who creates discomfort;
* Use reporting and blocking features; and
* Contact local emergency services where immediate help is required.

Icebreaker is not an emergency service and does not continuously monitor every user, message, or venue.
